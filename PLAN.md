# the plan for Dojo

## what is Dojo, to the user?

a one-stop collection of specialized interfaces for getting specific forms of information out of ones head and into something maximally legible to an agent as efficiently as possible

- UI sketching (highly opinionated TLDraw app) experience
  - basically exactly like `opencode-sketch` but with scene art and a hotkeys legend off to the side
- Agent-accessible Question Answering experience
- An interview scene (voice agent, transcript + ontology extraction + label + export)


other important characteristics include:

- *very* hotkey/APM/power-user oriented (i.e. actions and hotkeys and interaction patterns more generally are a major focus area)
- actually comes with a Training Mode, and users are encouraged to do the Training Mode for just 5 minutes a day
  - if you do 5 minutes a day for 7 days chances are high that it will stick
  - if you can recognize the utility potential from having Dojo stick, that initial investment is obviously worth it
  - it's actually kind of fun, too
  - the goal is to instill strong proficiency in both your ability to recall that this tool is available for your use and also your ability to use each interface well enough that you feel fluent and the experience is engaging
- totally free
- totally local
- BYOK for AI stuff
- actually feels like a video game. music, sound effects, scene art
  - think WC3:The Frozen Throne chains UI vibe
  - think Shado-pan Monastery (from WoW:MoP) art aesthetic


## what is Dojo, to the engineer?

- Foldkit + Electron app
- available as PWA, downloadable as Electron app if power user / want to get rid of the PWA browser header decoration for purely aesthetic reasons
- public open source repo

## why is Dojo

- between now (August 2026) and BCI maturity, there will be tremendous alpha in making tools for AI power users to up their brain-to-agent throughput
